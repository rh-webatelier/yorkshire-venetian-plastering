Six ways
to finish a wall

Each finish can be tinted to any colour and matched to your floors, joinery and lighting. Samples are made up before any work starts.

i

Venetian polished

Glass-smooth and gently marbled. Reflects light softly around hallways and living spaces.

ii

Metallic

Copper, bronze and gold worked into the plaster. Made for media walls and statement chimney breasts.

iii

Textured & stone

Travertine, raw stone and linear textures with real relief under a light.

iv

Microcement

Seamless, water-resistant walls and surfaces for bathrooms and wet areas.

v

Art walls & logos

Patterns, inlaid lines and logos built into the surface itself, not painted on top.

vi

Commercial

Hard-wearing finishes for receptions, bars, salons and showrooms, worked around opening hours.

The person who'll be at your wall

Yorkshire Venetian Plastering is a specialist, hands-on business. The person you speak to about your project is the person who mixes the colour, lays every coat and polishes the finish.

Trained in Italian techniques with Viero and Ardour Surface Design, and still adding new finishes to the range, from metallics to microcement.

From first visit to final polish

  1. 1

    Visit & samples

    We look at the space and the light, talk through finishes and colours, and make up samples for you to live with.

  2. 2

    Preparation

    Walls are protected, repaired and primed so the finish bonds properly and lasts.

  3. 3

    Layers by hand

    Thin coats are applied and worked with a steel trowel, building depth and movement one layer at a time.

  4. 4

    Burnish & seal

    The surface is polished to the sheen you chose, then sealed or waxed to protect it.
