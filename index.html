What is Venetian / polished plaster?

A hand-applied Italian plaster built up in fine layers and burnished to a smooth, marble-like finish with real depth — nothing like a painted wall.

What finishes can you do?

Marble effects, metallics (copper, bronze, gold), travertine and stone textures, and smooth polished finishes — in bespoke colours to suit your space.

Where do you work?

Leeds and across Yorkshire, for both homes and commercial spaces such as bars, salons and offices.

Do you do feature walls and media walls?

Yes — feature walls, media walls, fireplaces and full rooms, all finished seamlessly by hand.
